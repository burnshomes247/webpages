Background & Ongoing
CompassBackground

Compass's private network only works at the scale of its 35,000+ agents, creating a competitive moat smaller brokerages can't replicate.

IndustryBackground

Internal listing networks are only harmful at Compass's scale; the same tool at a small brokerage is just data management.

NAR / MLSBackground

NAR's Clear Cooperation Policy requires MLS submission within 1 business day of public marketing — the rule Compass's strategy is largely engineered to route around.

Reffkin / CompassBackground

Reffkin floated a national MLS concept, which critics read as a bid to replace the fragmented local MLS system with one Compass could more easily influence.

ZillowBackground

Zillow's core business depends on comprehensive listing inventory to drive buyer traffic it monetizes as agent leads — exactly what Compass's feed cut threatened.

ZillowBackground

Zillow's listing "Contact" button often routes buyers to a paid agent rather than the actual listing agent, without making that clear.

ZillowBackground

Zillow faces a separate suit over undisclosed referral fees paid by agents for buyer leads.

REMAXBackground

REMAX has aligned with Zillow's pre-marketing framework, positioning itself against Compass's private-network model.

Ricky CarruthBackground

Ricky Carruth's American Real Estate Association positions itself as an agent-first alternative to NAR and broker-driven policy debates.

IndustryBackground

Boero's proposed test: if you can't read a disclosure aloud to a client and have them understand what's happening, it fails — true of most contact-form and referral disclosures today.

Compass / ZillowBackground

Compass and Zillow both frame their strategies as consumer protection, but each also happens to serve its own commercial interest.

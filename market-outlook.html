Things to Watch
  • Increasing Ten-Year Treasury Bond Rate
    This is tied directly to mortgage rates and pushing them higher. Ideally, this number would be at 4.5% or below.
  • Tariff-driven inflation
    New tariffs on lumber, steel, and appliances are pushing prices higher across the board. Persistent inflation pressure makes it harder for the Fed to justify rate cuts, which keeps mortgage rates elevated for longer.
  • BLS Distress
    New BLS data confirms the DMV took the country's biggest employment hits over the past year from DOGE layoffs — six of the ten counties with the highest job-loss rates nationwide were in the D.C. metro area.
  • Inventory remains historically low
    Low inventory is helping keep prices in certain areas more stable than they would be otherwise.
